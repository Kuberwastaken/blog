Your timeline knows it's great. Every demo is really cool.

None of that matters because running it costs $30+ a day to run and 99% of the people geeking over it are not going to pay that (me included)

We're in a really fun phase with AI right now, as the subsidization for model costs decreases further, you're going to notice this pattern.

A powerful AI tool drops, there's twitter hype, developers (and normies) lose their minds, hobbyists max out credit cards, Your timeline is filled with miracles, and none of it matters.

People try to hack around it but inevitably, [AI is expensive](https://kuber.studio/blog/Reflections/The-Bubble-is-Not-What-You-Think) and it dies out soon, no matter how useful it is.

It's not all bad though. I'll be on eBay in three weeks trying to get cheap Mac minis from people panic-selling them :)

and then the next trend would come in
used, forgotten and onwards.
